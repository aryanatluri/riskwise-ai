The 5-Stage Enterprise Agentic Lifecycle

Why generic chatbots fail and how an Autonomous Action Agent transforms enterprise legal & compliance productivity.

1

Stage 1: Detect

Extracts verbatim factual clauses, page numbers, and exact terms from unstructured paperwork.

2

Stage 2: Understand

Performs tri-party benchmark against internal corporate policies and statutory regulations (DPDPA/CERT-In).

3

Stage 3: Decide

Computes mathematical Composite Risk Score (CRS = Impact × 0.6 + Likelihood × 0.4 × Multiplier).

4

Stage 4: Act

Writes legal redlines, pauses at Human-in-the-Loop review gate, and drafts vendor negotiation email.

5

Stage 5: Monitor
