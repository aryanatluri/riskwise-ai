MASTER SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT
1. SECURITY
1.1 Vendor shall notify Customer in writing within twelve (12) hours of becoming aware of any security incident affecting Customer data.
1.2 All Customer data at rest shall be encrypted using AES-256 with keys managed by Customer, and data in transit shall use TLS 1.3.
2. DATA LOCATION
2.1 Customer data, backups and logs shall be stored and processed solely within India. Vendor shall not transfer Customer data outside India without Customer's prior written approval.
3. AUDIT
3.1 Vendor shall permit Customer, its auditors and the Reserve Bank of India to audit and inspect Vendor's systems and records relating to the Services.
4. SUBCONTRACTING
4.1 Vendor may engage sub-processors only with Customer's prior written consent.
5. LIABILITY
5.1 The limitations of liability in this Section shall not apply to breaches of data protection or confidentiality obligations.
6. EXIT
6.1 On termination Vendor shall provide transition assistance and return of all Customer data, followed by certified deletion.
