SOFTWARE LICENCE AND SUPPORT AGREEMENT
1. SECURITY
1.1 Licensor shall encrypt data in transit using TLS 1.0 or higher and data at rest using AES-256.
1.2 Licensor shall notify Licensee of any security incident affecting Licensee data within 72 hours.
2. LIABILITY
2.1 Licensor's aggregate liability, including for data breach, shall be limited to USD 50,000.
3. TERMINATION
3.1 Upon termination, Licensor shall provide exit assistance for ninety days and return of Licensee data.
4. INSPECTION
4.1 Licensee and its regulators shall have the right to inspect Licensor's facilities.
